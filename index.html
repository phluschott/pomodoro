How it works

The basics

  • Start / Pause / Resume runs the clock. The spacebar does the same.
  • Reset stops the timer, goes back to Round 1 and clears your earned icons.
  • Skip jumps straight to the next phase. A skipped work period does not earn an icon.
  • A chime plays at the end of each phase. Turn it off with Chime at phase end.

Rounds and Sets

  • A Round is one work period plus the break after it.
  • A Set is a group of Rounds. Choose its length with Rounds before long break. After the last Round's work period you get the Long break, and the Set ends when that break ends.
  • Set Rounds before long break to 0 for endless Rounds with no long break.
  • The colored background and the big word under the clock show the current phase: Work, Rest or Long break.

Choose your split

  • Pick a preset: 25 / 5, 50 / 10, 52 / 17 or 90 / 20 (work minutes / rest minutes).
  • Pick Custom to type your own Work and Rest minutes.
  • Changing a time or the number of Rounds stops the timer and gives you a fresh work period. Your earned icons are kept.

Auto-start

  • A work period always flows into its break on its own.
  • Auto-start next Round: when a Round's break ends, the next work period starts by itself. Unchecked, the timer stops and waits for you to press Start.
  • Auto-start next Set: when the long break ends, the next Set starts by itself. Unchecked, the timer stops at the end of the Set. This option needs Rounds before long break to be 1 or more.

Round Awards

  • There is one square for every Round in the Set. Finish a work period and its square fills with your chosen award.
  • Complete the whole Set and every square is filled. They stay until you start the next Set.
  • Below the presets, pick which award you want. Your earned icons are saved, so they survive a page reload.

Colors and small windows

  • Pick one of ten color themes below the settings. Themes 1–8 are calm colors; 9 and 10 are dark “coder” themes. Each theme uses a different background for Work, Rest and Long break.
  • Make the window very small and the page shows just the clock and a round Play/Pause button. The background color still tells you the phase.
  • The time and phase also show in the browser tab title.
  • All of your choices are saved in this browser.
